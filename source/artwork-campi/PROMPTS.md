# Campi-artwork

Gemaakt met het ingebouwde imagegen-gereedschap. Stijlreferentie: het eerder goedgekeurde voorstel voor de KSF-ladder. PNG met transparante achtergrond; gebruikt op circa 240 × 160 px op desktop en 135 × 100 px op mobiel. Decoratief, dus lege alt-tekst.

## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create one small editorial spot illustration for a Dutch university educational website, a student holding a smartphone with abstract campus timetable blocks, beside a simple folded campus map and small campus building silhouette. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: match its delicate charcoal ink outlines, muted ochre gold, warm gray and tiny teal accents, subtle gouache and grainy paper texture INSIDE objects. Friendly mature illustration, simple silhouette readable at 160 pixels. Compact landscape composition, isolated on genuinely transparent background. No words, numbers, logos, badges, checkmarks, UI screenshot, or white rectangle. Only the illustration, ample transparent margin.
